BlockRise: Stack Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2025

This policy explains how BlockRise: Stack Rush (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the BlockRise mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and store the minimum data required to deliver the game experience:

  • Account & Profile Data: Optional username, avatar color, country selection, and gameplay stats (scores, level progression, achievements, coins).
  • Anonymous Identifiers: A Supabase-generated user ID so your progress can be saved securely.
  • Ad & Analytics Data: If you interact with ads, Google AdMob may collect device and ad engagement information per their own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • Sync and display your game progress, daily rewards, achievements, and leaderboard standing.
    • li>Detect fraud or abuse (for example, preventing fake high scores).
  • Show ads or reward videos when you choose to watch them.
  • Send optional reminders or notifications if you’ve granted permission.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on carefully selected partners to power parts of the experience:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and secure storage of your profile and game progress. Read their policy at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google AdMob: Delivers banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Capacitor / Ionic Native Plugins: Access to vibration, haptics, and local storage.

4. Data Retention & Security

  • Gameplay data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security) in the selected region.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Anonymous accounts can be deleted at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

5. Children’s Privacy

BlockRise is suitable for players aged 10+. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child provided personal information without consent, contact us so we can remove it.

6. Your Choices

  • Use the Settings screen to toggle notifications, theme, or vibration.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via your device settings (Google Play Services).
  • To delete your data, email us and include your username or user ID so we can locate the correct records.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this document to reflect new features or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date will change whenever we publish a revision. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions or data requests? Email twentyfimedia@gmail.com.
